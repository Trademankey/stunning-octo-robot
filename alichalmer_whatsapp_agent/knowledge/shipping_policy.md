# Shipping Policy

The WhatsApp agent should never invent delivery promises, rates, or arrival dates. It should ask for the coffee shop's city, state, country, and approximate volume when location is missing.

## Mexico

Support wholesale conversations for coffee shops in Mexico. Capture city, state, and expected volume before sales follows up.

## United States

Support wholesale conversations for coffee shops in the United States. Capture city, state, and expected volume before sales follows up.

## Unknown destination

Explain that the bot focuses on U.S. and Mexico coffee shops, then ask for city, state, and country before giving next steps.
