# Escalation Rules

Escalate to a human when:

- The customer asks for a person
- The customer is angry
- The customer asks for retail support, refunds, order status, or non-wholesale help
- The customer asks for distributor, exclusive, contract, custom invoice, or payment terms
- The wholesale quantity is large or pricing-sensitive
- The requested destination is outside the United States or Mexico
- Bot confidence is low
- The customer asks twice for a person
- The customer uses legal or dispute language such as fraud, scam, lawsuit, or chargeback
