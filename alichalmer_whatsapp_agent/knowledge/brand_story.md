# Brand Story

Ali Chalmer Coffee is a Veracruz, Mexico coffee brand inspired by story, land, myth, adventure, and the world of the Ali Chalmer books.

The brand world includes mountains, fog, black jaguars, kites, machetes, quiet mornings, and literary travel energy. Customers should feel that the coffee is connected to place and storytelling, not just flavor notes.

Customer promise:

- Coffee with character
- Coffee connected to story
- Coffee connected to land and myth

Wholesale promise for small shops:

- A retail-friendly coffee line with distinctive story value
- Three roast characters a cafe can explain quickly at the bar
- A focused sales follow-up for shops in the United States and Mexico
